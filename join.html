Join BAHE

BAHE is a collaborative research group exploring biodiversity, wildlife health, ecological interactions and socioecological systems through rigorous, curiosity-driven science.

We welcome researchers driven by curiosity about the natural world and motivated to tackle important ecological questions through rigorous science. BAHE brings together researchers with diverse expertise—including field ecology, molecular biology, quantitative ecology, modelling and interdisciplinary synthesis, and we believe that this diversity of approaches strengthens both our science and our community. We encourage researchers to develop their own ideas, build on their individual strengths and ambitions, and contribute to a collaborative environment where excellence, integrity and mutual support enable everyone to achieve more than they could alone.

Opportunities

Postdoctoral Fellows

Develop independent research, build international collaborations and prepare competitive fellowship applications while contributing to ambitious ecological research.

PhD Researchers

Undertake original research addressing fundamental ecological questions in biodiversity, wildlife ecology, One Health, ecological interactions and quantitative ecology.

MRes Students

Gain intensive research experience within an active research environment and develop the skills needed for doctoral research or careers in ecology, conservation and quantitative biology.

Visiting Researchers

Collaborate on existing projects, develop new ideas and build international partnerships through short- or longer-term research visits.

Our research culture

BAHE exists to generate scientific knowledge that advances ecological understanding and supports biodiversity conservation and sustainable futures. Achieving this requires curiosity, creativity, analytical rigour and a sustained commitment to scientific excellence.

We seek researchers who are ambitious, intellectually engaged and motivated to tackle important ecological questions. Scientific potential is not defined by career stage or existing expertise, but by curiosity, a willingness to think critically and the determination to transform good questions into robust scientific knowledge. In return, BAHE provides an environment where independence, collaboration and strong mentorship help ideas mature into meaningful scientific contributions. Ultimately, our measure of success is not simply the researchers who leave BAHE, but the lasting contributions they make to the cumulative growth of scientific knowledge.

We recognise that the strongest science does not always produce convenient answers. We encourage researchers to follow evidence with intellectual honesty, even when it challenges established assumptions or reveals uncomfortable realities, because robust knowledge provides the foundation for informed decisions, biodiversity conservation and sustainable futures.

Who thrives at BAHE?

Researchers who thrive at BAHE are curious enough to ask important questions, rigorous enough to answer them well, and ambitious enough to ensure that their work contributes to the cumulative growth of ecological knowledge. They value integrity over convenience, evidence over opinion, and collaboration over individual achievement because they recognise that meaningful science is built collectively. They are prepared to question assumptions, embrace uncertainty and pursue unexpected discoveries, recognising that scientific progress often begins where existing explanations no longer provide satisfactory answers.

Researchers are encouraged to develop deep expertise in their own disciplines while recognising that the most influential science emerges when complementary strengths are integrated to answer questions that no single approach could resolve alone.
