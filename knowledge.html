Knowledge, Learning and Engagement

How BAHE research is developed, shared and connected through people, ideas and communities.

Knowledge grows where people, ideas and evidence meet

Science is a collective endeavour. Through research, teaching, collaboration, critical discussion and engagement, people build on one another’s ideas and evidence to deepen understanding, inspire future generations and help shape more sustainable futures.

Selected activities, milestones and research resources illustrate how knowledge is generated, shared, challenged and applied across the BAHE Knowledge Network.
