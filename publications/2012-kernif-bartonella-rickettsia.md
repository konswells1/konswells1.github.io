# Concise publication summary.
summary: >
  This empirical study investigated Bartonella and Rickettsia in fleas, ticks
  and lice collected from domestic animals in the Lao PDR and Malaysian
  Borneo using molecular detection methods. The study documents widespread
  Rickettsia felis infection in flea populations, identifies Bartonella
  clarridgeiae in a subset of fleas, and provides molecular evidence for a
  potentially novel Rickettsia associated with cattle ticks.

# Knowledge-network summary.
knowledge_summary: >
  The study expands understanding of arthropod-borne bacterial pathogens in
  Southeast Asia by demonstrating that domestic animal ectoparasites harbour
  multiple zoonotic bacteria with distinct prevalence patterns. It identifies
  both Ctenocephalides felis felis and Ctenocephalides orientis as carriers of
  Rickettsia felis, documents occasional co-infection with Bartonella
  clarridgeiae, and reports molecular evidence consistent with a previously
  undescribed Rickettsia lineage in Boophilus ticks. The findings highlight the
  value of molecular surveillance of ectoparasites for detecting recognised
  and emerging vector-borne pathogens.

# Scientific or societal significance.
impact_statement: >
  The study provides important baseline evidence on the distribution of
  zoonotic bacterial pathogens in arthropod vectors in Southeast Asia and
  identifies candidate novel Rickettsia diversity that warrants further
  isolation, taxonomic investigation and epidemiological study.

# Non-technical summary.
plain_language_summary: >-
  Researchers examined fleas, ticks and lice collected from domestic animals
  in Laos and Malaysian Borneo for bacteria that can cause disease. Many fleas
  carried Rickettsia felis, a smaller number carried Bartonella clarridgeiae,
  and cattle ticks contained DNA from a Rickettsia that may represent a new
  species. These findings improve understanding of disease-causing bacteria
  circulating in common animal parasites in Southeast Asia.
